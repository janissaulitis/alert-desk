Hunting query to start with (Microsoft Defender Advanced Hunting, KQL)

DeviceFileEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(6h)
| where DeviceName =~ "FIN-LT-07"
| where ActionType in ("FileRenamed", "FileModified")
| summarize Files = count() by bin(Timestamp, 5m), InitiatingProcessFileName
| order by Timestamp asc
